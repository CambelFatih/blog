WALDEN

Henry David Thoreau

ECONOMY

When I wrote the following pages, or rather the Bulk of them, I lived alone, in the woods, a mile from any neighbor, in a house which I had built myself, on the shore of Walden Pond, in Concord, Massachusetts, and earned my living by the labor of my hands only. I lived there two years and two months. At present I am a sojourner in Civilized life again. I should not obtrude my affairs so much on the notice of my readers if very particular inquiries had not been made by my townsmen concerning my mode of life, which some would call impertinent, though they do not appear to me at all impertinent, but, considering the circumstances, very natural and pertinent. Some have asked what I got to eat; if I did not feel lonesome; if I was not afraid; and the like. Others have been curious to learn what portion of my income I devoted to charitable purposes; and some, who have large families, how many poor children I maintained. I will therefore ask those of my readers who feel no particular interest in me to pardon me if I undertake to answer some of these questions in this book. In most books, the I, or first person, is omitted; in this it will be retained; that, in respect to egotism, is the main difference. Moreover, I, on my side, require of every writer, first or last, a simple and sincere account of his own life, and not merely what he has heard of other men's lives; some such account as he would send to his kindred from a distant land; for if he has lived sincerely, it must have been in a distant land to me. Perhaps these pages are more particularly addressed to poor students. As for the rest of my readers, they will accept such portions as apply to them. I trust that none will stretch the seams in putting on the coat, for it may do good service to him whom it fits. I would fain say something, not so much concerning the Chinese and Sandwich Islanders as you who read these pages, who are said to live in New England; something about your condition, especially your outward condition or circumstances in this world, in this town, what it is, whether it is necessary that it be as bad as it is, whether it cannot be improved as well as not. I have travelled a good deal in Concord; and everywhere, in shops, and offices, and fields, the inhabitants have appeared to me to be doing penance in a thousand remarkable ways. What I have heard of Bramins sitting exposed to four fires and looking in the face of the sun; or hanging suspended, with their heads downward, over flames; or looking at the heavens over their shoulders "until it becomes impossible for them to resume their natural position, while from the twist of the neck nothing but liquids can pass into the stomach"; or dwelling, chained for life, at the foot of a tree; or measuring with their bodies, like caterpillars, the breadth of vast empires; or standing on one leg on the tops of pillars-even these forms of conscious penance are hardly more incredible and astonishing than the scenes which I daily witness. Bu metin, Ralph Waldo Emerson'un "Self-Reliance" adlı denemesinden bir alıntıdır. Emerson, burada kişisel deneyimlerinin kısıtlılığına işaret ederek, bir yazarın kendi hayatından samimi bir hesap vermesini, başkalarının hayatları hakkında duyduklarından daha önemli gördüğünü belirtir. Emerson, okuyucularına özellikle fakir öğrencileri hedef alarak, hayatları hakkında samimi bir hesap vermesi gerektiğini söyler. Ayrıca, özellikle New England'da yaşayan okuyucularına, dış koşulları ve şartları hakkında düşünmeleri gerektiğini belirtir. Concord'da seyahat ettiği süre boyunca, insanların kendilerine acı çektirdiklerini ve zorlu hayat koşulları altında yaşadıklarını gözlemlediğini ifade eder. Emerson, insanların kendi kendilerine yeterli olmaları gerektiğine inanan bir filozoftur. Bu metin de, insanların kendi hayatlarına odaklanmaları ve kendi gerçekliklerini yaratmaları gerektiğini vurgulayan felsefesine atıfta bulunur. Ayrıca, insanların toplumsal baskılardan ve geleneksel düşüncelerden kurtulmaları gerektiğini savunur. Sonuç olarak, bu metin, okuyucuları kendi hayatlarına ve koşullarına odaklanmaya, başkalarının hayatları hakkında duydukları hikayeler yerine kendi hayat hikayelerine daha fazla değer vermeye teşvik eder. Ayrıca, insanların kendilerine yeterli olduğunu ve kendi gerçekliklerini yaratabileceklerini vurgular.

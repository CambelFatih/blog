Chapter 1

The Battle of the Fangs

A large pack of wolves ran quickly among the snowdrifts. At the front of the pack ran a big grey wolf. He was the leader of the pack and the strongest wolf among them. It was he who began to run faster and then looked at the she-wolf. He did not snarl at her when she tried to outrun him. On the contrary, he wanted to run near to her. But on her other side ran a strong, old wolf. His muzzle and body had the scars of many battles. He had only one eye. He always ran on her right side. At such times the old wolf did his best to touch her body, shoulder, or neck. But the she-wolf did not like this. She rejected his attention with her angry snarl and sharp teeth every time. The situation in the pack was desperate. The lack of food and risinganger went together. The wolves were very weak and thin. But they ran as quickly as they could. At the back of the pack ran the old wolves, and at the front were the strongest ones. They ran many miles that day without stopping. They ran in a frozen and dead world. Nothing moved. They alone were alive, and they were looking for anything else that could survive the harsh conditions. Luckily, they found a dead moose in the snow. They ate it at once. After that they slept for a long time with their full stomachs. The hunger was over. The next morning the pack ran down to the Mackenzie River. Each day one or two wolves died on the way. In the end, there were only three wolves left: the she-wolf, the one-eyed wolf, and the leader of the pack. The she-wolf was very angry at the time. Both the old wolf and young wolf had the marks of her teeth on them. They were kind to the she-wolf, but they were very angry at one another.

One day, the leader of the pack and the one-eyed wolf began to fight to the death. They were fighting for the love of the she-wolf. In the meantime, the she-wolf stood still and watched. She was even glad. This was her day. The one-eyed wolf was clever in battle. The leader turned his head to lick a wound on his shoulder. His neck was open to his wise enemy. At that moment the one-eyed wolf saw the opportunity. He ran up to his young enemy and closed his fangs on his neck. He killed the leader of the pack easily. The whole time the she-wolf just sat on the snow and smiled. She was glad at the tragedy of the natural world, that was a tragedy to those who died. When the leader of the pack lay in the snow and did not move, One Eye went up to the she-wolf. His movement was full of triumph and caution. This time she did not bite him with her sharp teeth. She began to lead him through the snowy forest. After that they always ran side by side, like good friends. They hunted and ate their meat together. The days passed by quickly. They did not remain in one place, but travelled across the country until they reached the Mackenzie River. Sometimes they met other wolves on their way, but there was no friendship or gladness in those meetings. It was near a small stream that flowed into the Mackenzie River. The she-wolf walked slowly and carefully. One Eye walked behind her. Soon she reached a little cave near the bank. She stopped near the cave and smelled it attentively. Then she entered the cave alone. The cave was dry and comfortable.

"Wound" ve "scar" terimleri, ciltteki yaralanmaları tanımlamak için kullanılır."Wound", bir cilt yaralanması veya hasarı anlamına gelir. Yara genellikle cildin dış katmanlarındaki hasarlarla ilişkilidir ve cilt yüzeyinde açık bir yara veya kanama ile sonuçlanabilir. Wound, kesi, yırtık, yanık, ezik veya sıyrık gibi çeşitli şekillerde olabilir. Buna karşılık, "scar" yara izi anlamına gelir. Bir yaralanma iyileştikten sonra, vücut yaralı bölgeyi tamir etmek için yeni bir cilt dokusu üretir. Bu yeni dokunun görünümü, yaralanmanın boyutu, derinliği ve yaralanmanın yerine bağlı olarak değişebilir. Bazı durumlarda, yeni cilt dokusu yara izi olarak kalabilir ve bu izler "scar" olarak adlandırılır. Yani temel fark, "wound" ciltteki bir yaralanmayı, "scar" ise bir yara sonrasında oluşan izi ifade eder.

Chapter 2

The Grey Cub

The grey cub was different from his brothers and sisters. They inherited the red colour of their fur from the she-wolf, He alone inherited the grey colour from his father. He looked like a real wolf. He knew his sisters and brothers very well. He played with them clumsily and slowly. He learned his mother by touch and smell. Most of the first month of his life he slept for a long time. But after a few months, he began to learn about his new world with great curiosity. Actually, his world was very small. Its limits were the walls of the cave. Still, he saw the bright light of the world outside. He always tried to reach this light by crawling toward the entrance of the cave. And his brothers and sisters followed his lead. Unfortunately, their mother always pushed them back from the entrance. He was an angry little cub. It was normal because he was a predator. His mother and father ate only meat. Yet he could snarl much louder than his brothers and sisters. It was he that first bit another cub by the ear angrily. And it was he that caused the mother the biggest trouble in the cave. The grey cub's desire for light grew from day to day. He always wanted to crawl towards the cave's entrance. Unfortunately, his mother pushed him back every time. The any other place except for the cave. So to him the entrance of the cave was a wall of light. The light attracted him as a candle attracts flies. The cub knew that it was the way out. Nevertheless, he himself did not know anything about it. He did not know the outside world at all. The grey cub knew his father, too. He was a wolf like his mother, who slept near

Like most animals of the Wild, he faced hunger early on. His father stopped bringing meat, and his mother had no milk after some time. The cubs cried with hunger all day. Then the cubs slept. The life that was in them grew weaker every moment. Some of them never woke up again. One Eye was desperate. He did his best to find a prey. The she-wolf, too, left her lair and went out in search of meat. When the grey cub woke up he noticed that his only sister was dead. His sister no longer lifted her head nor moved cheerfully. He could not understand what had happened. Then there came a time when his father disappeared once and for all. The she-wolf knew why One Eye never came back. He had died in battle with a wild lynx. She saw his dead body near the lynx's lair up the stream. But she did not try to fight with the lynx that had many hungry kittens. Motherhood is motherhood. Besides, the lynx was much stronger and angrier during a fight. When his mother left the cave to hunt the grey cub never tried to leave the cave. It was a rule he would not break. So he just slept or waited for his mother. He did not leave the cave because of fear as well. He learnt it from One Eye and the she-wolf. So the grey cub knew fear, the heritage of the Wild which no animal can escape. Instinct needed law and control. But growth needed disobedience. That is why one day the grey cub crawled toward the entrance of the cave when his mother was not there. His fear was gone and only interest to live. The next moment he was in the outside world. It was strange. The light was very bright. Fear made him go back, but youth pushed him forward. He could not see anything for a while.

Then he saw many huge objects all around. Everything was unknown to him at the time. He was very scared. He looked out at the world attentively. Nothing happened. He continued to look for some time. After that he walked forward slowly. The grey cub lived all his life on a dry and level ground. Suddenly, he rolled down the hill. He never felt the hurt of a fall. He was in a panic. The unknown caught him at last. But then he got up and looked at the things in the forest with curiosity. He smelled the grass and the plants that grew among the trees. Then he began to run after a squirrel in the forest joyfully. The little cub was learning. He ran among many things. There were live things and things that did not move. The things that were not alive remained always still, but the live things moved without stopping. He ran very clumsily. He was very joyful and curious. He met many different animals on his way. He had learned so much already. He no longer was afraid of anything. He forgot all about the unknown. Then he came down to the stream. He had never seen the water before. The ground looked good near the stream. He stepped bravely into it. The next moment he cried with fear. It was very cold. He came back to land quickly and did not step into the water again. gave him the feeling of the unknown, that meant danger and pain. He had learned something more about the world. Water was not alive. Yet it moved like live things. His discovery was that things were not always what they seemed to be. That is why the grey cub would have to learn the reality of a thing before he could believe it. After his adventures, the grey cub decided to return to his cave near the stream.

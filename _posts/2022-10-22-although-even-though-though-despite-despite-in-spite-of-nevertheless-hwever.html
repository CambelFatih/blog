Though=rağmen+Cümlecik
Although=rağmen+Cümlecik
Even though=rağmen+Cümlecik

Hava soğuk olmasına rağmen yürüyüşe gittik.
Although the weather was cold, we went jogging.
We went jogging even though the weather was cold.
